Exceptional Architectural Achievement

Welcome to The Obsidian Penthouse, a triumph of contemporary design and engineering. Occupying the entire top three floors of the prestigious Obsidian Tower, this residence offers unparalleled 360-degree views of the Atlantic Ocean and the Lagos city skyline.

Designed by world-renowned architects, the interior showcases a seamless blend of Italian marble, smoked oak, and floor-to-ceiling structural glass. The triple-height living area creates a sense of scale rarely found in private residences, while the private elevator provides discreet access to all levels, including the rooftop sky lounge and infinity pool.
